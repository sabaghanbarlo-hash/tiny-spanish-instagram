# Tiny Spanish: posting plan

One new reel per day from this folder, in file-name order. `daily-refill.yml` (06:30 UTC) moves the next scripts into the queue, renders them, and keeps the queue 5 days ahead. `daily-reel.yml` (12:45 UTC) posts the next one to Instagram with the `.caption` as the description.
To add a reel: drop in `NAME.txt` + `NAME.caption`. To reorder: rename (the 12-digit prefix decides the order). The workflow warns when 7 or fewer scripts are left.

## Why this series
Travel-situation phrases ("say this when...") get saved and sent to friends, which are the signals Reels reward. Same engine as Tiny Japanese: hook first line, 3 phrases, save + send CTA, 5 hashtags.

## Posting times (UTC)
| Time | ET | Post |
|---|---|---|
| 12:45 | 7:45 am | daily story reel (this folder) |
| 17:45 | 12:45 pm | practice reel (`bank-practice/`) |
| 23:45 | 6:45 pm | mistake and fix reel (`bank-mistake/`) |

Public 2026 posting-time studies disagree on exact hours (Social Pilot and Wednesday-morning data favor about 7 am ET; Later and HubSpot put education at 4 pm and 8 pm). These three slots cover the morning, lunch and evening windows. Check Instagram Insights after 4 weeks and shift the slot that performs best.
