[grammar]
grammar: Soy / eres (from ser) = I am / you are. Use it for who you are and where you're from. Spanish usually drops "yo" and "tú": Soy Emma is enough.
example: Soy Emma. | I'm Emma.
example: Eres de México. | You're from Mexico.

[quiz]
quiz: How do you say "I'm from Chicago"? | Soy de Chicago | Me llamo Chicago | Eres de Chicago | A | Soy de + place = I'm from + place.
